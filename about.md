# Dear Power

This is an open letter to those in power. Honest feedback, addressed — not
negotiated. One-directional by default: an *address*, not a bargain. If a
conversation happens, good; but it isn't the point.

The point is simpler, and it's said with respect: **most people in power have
no real idea what they're doing** — not because they're stupid or venal, but
because the job is genuinely hard and *they get almost no honest feedback*. I've
been in far simpler roles and didn't have the clue either. From the outside,
some of it is just easier to see. So I'll say it plainly.

I don't write this expecting to fix anyone in power. I write it because the
feedback helps **us** — the people who *do* see it and are willing to say it, in
as many forums as it takes. A society only gets less stupid when its feedback
loops actually carry signal. Building those loops, out loud, is the work.

I write from inside the system, not against it — because tearing the loops down
breaks things worth keeping. The north star is further out than this blog: a
politics organized from the bottom up, on feedback that scales as the world gets
complex. This is one small loop toward it.

Plainly, and with respect.

— *Dear Power*
